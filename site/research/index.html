RESEARCH / READING GUIDE

What does a model keep, and what can it use?

This investigation follows the gap between information stored in a model and information used to make a prediction.

Current finding: On one frozen revision-branch benchmark, a small external readout recovered a distinction that the model’s own prediction layer barely used. This does not yet show that replacing that layer improves general next-byte prediction.

Open question: Can a small replacement consumer turn that available information into better predictions? Here, “consumer” means the operation that reads the model’s internal state and produces its output.
