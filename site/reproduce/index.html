COMPILED EXPERIMENTS

Research an agent can pick up.

A Compiled Experiment turns a research claim into a portable, machine-readable object: the hypothesis, task and data semantics, protocol, environment, implementation references, evidence, and qualification receipts needed to reproduce the work independently.

Download one ZIP, hand it to an agent, and ask it to inspect the crate, reconstruct the declared reproduction environment, execute the included validation/reproduction protocol, and compare its evidence against the frozen claim.
